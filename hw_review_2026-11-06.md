## P0 – Fixes

### P0-1 · ESP32-S3 EN (CHIP_PU) has no pull-up ⚠️
- **Where:** Microcontroler page, U6 pin 3, net with C48 (1 µF), C47 (100 nF), SW1, TP9
- **Problem:** EN has only capacitors and the reset button, with no resistor to 3.3 V. Espressif requires that CHIP_PU never float and recommends an RC delay of 10 kΩ + 1 µF.
- **Fix:** add **10 kΩ from EN to 3.3 V** (0603, Basic part). The 1 µF is already there.
- **V1.0 patch:** solder a 10 kΩ between TP9 and the nearest 3.3 V pad.
- [ ] Schematic + PCB

### P0-2 · I²C bus on ESP32-S3 strapping pins IO0 and IO45 ⚠️
- **Where:** SDA = IO0 (U6 pin 27), SCL = IO45 (U6 pin 26), with 6.8 kΩ pull-ups R21/R22 on the Amplifier page
- **Problem:**
  - IO45 selects the VDD_SPI voltage at reset (high = 1.8 V). The SCL pull-up holds it high. On the N8R8 (3.3 V flash/PSRAM), boot only works if the module's eFuse overrides this strap, and that isn't guaranteed across module batches.
  - IO0 with a pull-up is fine for normal boot. But any I²C device holding SDA low at reset sends the ESP32 into download mode.
- **Fix:** move SDA/SCL to free non-strapping GPIOs (for example IO13/IO14, or IO21 + another free pin) and update the firmware pin map.
- **V1.0 check:** run `espefuse.py summary` on every board and confirm the VDD_SPI_FORCE / VDD_SPI_TIEH settings. Flag any board without the override.
- [ ] Schematic + PCB + firmware

### P0-3 · Output inductors: two footprints in parallel per leg
- **Where:** Amplifier page. L7‖L8 (OUTA+→A+), L9‖L10 (OUTA−→A−), L11‖L12 (OUTB+→B+), L13‖L14 (OUTB−→B−)
- **Problem:** each leg has an XGL1712-103 **and** an MA5172-AE connected between the same nets. If both are fitted, each leg is 5 µH instead of 10 µH, and the filter corner and ripple current move.
- **Fix:** keep **only the MA5172-AE footprint** and delete L7, L9, L11, L13 (XGL1712). See P1-1 for the reasons.
- **V1.0 check:** confirm that only one inductor per leg is fitted on existing boards.
- [ ] Schematic + PCB

### P0-4 · Inrush current: 6600 µF on PVDD behind a fast-acting fuse
- **Where:** C4 (Power 12V page) + C54, C55 (Amplifier page) = 3 × 2200 µF / 63 V; F1 = Littelfuse 0451005 (fast-acting); Q1 reverse-polarity P-FET turns on within a fraction of a millisecond
- **Problem:** hot-plugging a stiff supply can blow the fuse or slowly weaken it.
- **Fix (pick one or more):**
  - [ ] Add **100 nF–1 µF from Q1 gate to source**, so Q1 ramps on slowly (soft-start)
  - [ ] Replace F1 with a **time-lag (slow-blow)** fuse in the same NANO² footprint
  - [ ] Reduce the bulk capacitance (see P1-3)

### P0-5 · Verify before the next order (no change expected, just confirm)
- [ ] **TVS D2 (SMCJ36A) vs PVDD nominal.** If PVDD is close to 36 V, the TVS leaks or heats at the top of the supply tolerance. Choose the TVS for the real maximum PVDD.
- [ ] **LMQ66430 pin 13 = MODE/SYNC** (U1 and U3) is tied to 3.3 V. Confirm in the datasheet variant table that "high" means **FPWM** for `LMQ66430MC3RXBRQ1`. If not, the bucks run PFM at light load, which can cause audible-band ripple and coil whine.
- [ ] **TPS7A4701 ANY-OUT pins** (U2: pins 5, 6, 7, 9 grounded; 4, 8, 10 open). Confirm 12.0 V on TP3 (12V_GVDD).
- [ ] **Output film caps:** are C98/C102/C90/C94 (150 nF) and C99/C103/C91/C95 (220 nF) meant to be fitted together with the 680 nF, about 1.05 µF per leg? Or are they alternative footprints? Decide and document.

## P1 – Savings

### How to move parts to cheaper options in the JLCPCB flow

1. **Know the part classes.** In the JLCPCB parts library, **Basic** parts are preloaded and have no per-part loading fee. **Extended** parts carry a feeder-loading fee per *unique* part number. Check jlcpcb.com/parts for the current fee rules and the "Preferred Extended" category.
2. **Find the Extended parts.** In EasyEDA Pro, export the BOM and, in the JLCPCB part library search, filter by **"Basic"**. Any LCSC number not found under Basic is Extended.
3. **Replace in EasyEDA Pro.** Select the component → *Replace Component* (or right-click → *Update/Replace*) → in the library panel choose *JLCPCB Assembly / LCSC*, filter for **Basic**, and match value, footprint, tolerance and voltage. To change every instance of a part at once, use *Find Similar Objects* → multi-select → replace.
4. **Consolidate values.** Fewer unique part numbers means fewer Extended fees. Use one 1 µF part, one 100 nF part and one resistor brand for everything.
5. **Mark hand-soldered parts** (MA5172, film caps, bulk electrolytics, terminal blocks, headers) as *Exclude from BOM / Do not place* for JLC assembly, so they aren't quoted or sourced.
6. **Re-run DRC** after footprint changes, regenerate the BOM and CPL, and check the part rotations in JLC's preview before ordering.

### P1-1 · Output inductors: keep MA5172-AE, drop XGL1712-103
| | MA5172-AE | XGL1712-103MED |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Powdered-iron toroid, designed for TI class-D EVMs | Molded composite, designed for DC-DC |
| L tolerance | ±1 % | ±20 % |
| DCR | 26 mΩ max | ~4.4 mΩ max |
| Isat | 45 A | 14.3 A (10 % drop) |
| Price @1 | ~$7.13 | ~$9.97 |

## P2 – Improvements

### P2-1 · Filter the analog LDO input (TAS3251 AVDD)
- **Where:** U5 TPS7A2033, fed from +5V, which is shared with two switching regulators
- **Change:** add a **ferrite bead (0603, ~600 Ω @ 100 MHz) + 4.7–10 µF** between +5V and U5's IN. Return the new capacitor to **GND** on the bead's input side; keep C42 on **AGND**.
- [ ] Schematic + PCB

### P2-2 · Analog input coupling capacitors
- **Where:** C57–C60 (10 µF X7R 25 V 1210) between the DAC output network and the power-stage inputs
- **Change:** 50 V X7R in the same 1210 footprint (much less capacitance loss under bias), or film / bipolar electrolytic if THD measurements show a rise at low frequencies
- [ ] Measure THD vs frequency before and after

### P2-3 · +5V rail is ~4.6 V (diode OR-ing D5/D3)
- **Option A:** R9 12.4 k → **11.3 k** (≈5.4 V before the diode, ≈5.0 V after)
- **Option B:** move the top of the feedback divider (R8) to the +5V side of D5, so the loop corrects for the diode drop
- **Option C:** ideal-diode / P-FET OR-ing (no drop)
- **Side effect:** with a true 5.0 V, the SK9822's VIH ≈ 3.5 V > 3.3 V GPIO. Add a **74AHCT1G125** (or similar) on the data and clock lines, or keep the LEDs on the diode-dropped rail.
- Also gives the AP63203 (3.3 V buck) more input headroom.
- [ ] Decide on an option

### P2-4 · Add a BOOT button on IO0
- After P0-2 frees IO0: add a button from IO0 to GND (+ optional 100 nF) for recovery or download mode when USB auto-download fails.
- [ ] Schematic + PCB

### P2-5 · Buck converter mode and noise
- If P0-5 shows the LMQ66430s are **not** in FPWM: use the FPWM variant or drive MODE/SYNC correctly.
- TPS7A4701 is premium for a gate-driver rail. Consider a cheaper LDO **only after** FPWM is confirmed.
- [ ] Depends on P0-5

### P2-6 · Output filter
- Decide on the final per-leg capacitance (680 nF ≈ 60 kHz corner vs ~1.05 µF ≈ 49 kHz with 10 µH) and document the reason.
- If the MA5172 buzzes mechanically: fix it with a dab of glue or varnish rather than changing the part.
- [ ] Measure frequency response into 4 Ω / 8 Ω / open

- IO3 goes to H2 (JTAG source strap). Same note.
